6×6 Mini Sudoku

Fill in the grid so that every row, column, and 2×3 block contains numbers 1 through 6 without duplicates.

How to Play
  • Numbers 1–6: Only enter digits between 1 and 6 into empty cells.
  • Rows & Columns: Every horizontal row and vertical column must contain all 6 digits exactly once.
  • 2×3 Blocks: Each framed sub-grid block of 6 cells must also contain digits 1–6.
